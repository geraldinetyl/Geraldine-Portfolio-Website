The Brief

During my internship at Vintedge, I supported the creative team in developing and adapting digital marketing assets for LG Electronics. The work involved creating social media visuals across different product campaigns while ensuring each asset remained consistent with the brand’s visual direction and campaign requirements.

The Approach

Working within an established brand system required balancing creativity with consistency. I focused on clear visual hierarchy, product emphasis, layout adaptation and platform-specific formatting so that each asset could communicate effectively across Facebook, Instagram and motion-based content.

Design Considerations

Each asset had to work within existing campaign and brand guidelines while still being clear, engaging and platform-appropriate. This meant considering product placement, headline hierarchy, legibility, format restrictions and how the visual would appear within fast-moving social feeds.

Commercial Workflow

The internship strengthened my understanding of producing design work within a professional marketing environment. I learnt how to adapt assets efficiently, organise working files, respond to feedback and prepare visuals for different social and digital touchpoints.

Outcome & Reflection

This internship gave me practical exposure to commercial design workflows and the process of working with an established brand. Beyond creating visuals, I gained a stronger understanding of how digital campaign assets are adapted across multiple formats while maintaining consistency, clarity and brand alignment.

The experience also helped me become more confident in working quickly, receiving feedback and translating campaign objectives into visual assets across static and motion-based formats.
